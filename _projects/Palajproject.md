The project's goal is to help participants learn how to conduct research, conduct fieldwork, engage with the public, build people-centric survey forms based on the problem's chosen frame, and propose solutions that are appropriate and accepted by the group.

Multiple extensive fieldworks were carried out to better understand the community issues and identify the most prevalent one that the majority of them were facing.

Created a survey form and gathered data from a wide number of people in the community who might be able to assist us in finding solutions.

After reviewing the collected data and performing some desk research on the most feasible solutions that fit the local context, recommend suitable solutions for the problem.

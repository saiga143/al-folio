Analyzed the importance of why energy transition is required from non-renewable to renewable sources and evaluated different renewable sources relevant to Enschede, The Netherlands.

Assessed the potential of Enschede to produce electricity through rooftop solar installations following a custom methodology and specific assumptions.

Developed a web application giving details about each building of Enschede like how many panels can be installed, how much power can be produced, the investment required, and how much CO2 can be reduced by producing electricity through solar.

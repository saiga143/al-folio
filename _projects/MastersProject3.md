Built a sustainability framework for the context of Enschede city by applying theoretical concepts.

Using primary and secondary data, an assessment of the sustainability of the city Enschede was made using appropriate indicators according to the developed framework and criteria.

Spatial and statistical methods were used to analyze and compare the dynamics and geographical patterns of sustainable urban development.

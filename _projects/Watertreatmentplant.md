The design project involves submitting a brief plan with all the necessary calculations and different plant elements with dimensions that are required to set up a functional drinking water treatment plant in a city. The chosen case study area for this project is Hyderabad, India.

The project involves estimating the per capita water demand for the city as well as designing appropriate rapid mixing tank, flocculation tank, sedimentation tank, rapid sand filter, disinfection and chemical house.

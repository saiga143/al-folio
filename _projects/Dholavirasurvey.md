Mapped the terrain of an ancient Harrapan civilization site named Dholavira in the state of Gujarat, India using different surveying equipment's like Total station, Auto Level, Real time kinematic (RTK) and Terrestrial laser scanning.

Estimated the volume of reservoirs, mapped the drainage network, structure of the citadel area using data points collected through different surveying equipment using ArcGIS.

The project's purpose is to offer market trends in domestic freight movement of USA to a startup so that it can decide what modes of transportation and types of freight to begin its operations with.

- Through data analysis and visualization tools like Flourish & Tableau insights have been provided on (i) which transport modes should they invest in? (ii) which freight category and commodities should they consider? (iii) which origin-destination pairs should they focus on for combinations of different transportation modes and freight categories.

- Individual assessments and user testings were conducted to test the usability and applicability of the visualizations in order to better understand how users feel about them and improve them.

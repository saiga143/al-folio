Studied urban heat island effect for the city of Ahmedabad using Landsat imagery by calculating land surface temperature (LST) and vegetation using NDVI parameter derived from DN values.

Analysis was made to check the urban heat island effect for the city over the past 2 decades and discovered that the UHI effect over the city is increasing at an alarming rate.

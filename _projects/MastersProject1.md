Socio-vulnerability assessment and mapping of Envigado by building a methodological approach and appropriate rationale for each chosen socio-economic indicator.

Modeled urban growth scenarios for the city of Envigado for the year 2030 and developed alternative urban development plans which mitigate the impacts resulting from the urban growth scenarios.

Evaluated the alternative plans in terms of their social and environmental impacts in relation to the modeled urban growth scenarios.

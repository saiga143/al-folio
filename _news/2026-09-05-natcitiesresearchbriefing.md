Nature Cities has published an easier-to-digest Research Briefing to accompany our recent paper on hidden living-condition deficits in small and medium cities.

Available to read for free at this link: [https://rdcu.be/KTC2SKiTNEAa](https://rdcu.be/KTC2SKiTNEAa)

Our neighborhood-scale model
estimates that about 395 million
people across 103 countries in
Africa, Asia, and Latin America
and the Caribbean live in
morphologically deprived
areas — neighborhoods that
are densely built, cramped and
poorly connected by roads. More
than one-third of these people
live in small- and medium-sized
cities, which reveals deprivation
beyond the megacities that
dominate global attention.

Many thanks to the Nature editorial team for the invitation to contribute to this initiative and for helping make the work more accessible and to Dana Thomson for co-authoring the briefing with me.

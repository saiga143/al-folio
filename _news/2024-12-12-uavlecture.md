A First Experience to Remember! 🚀

Last week, I had the opportunity to deliver my first-ever lecture (1hr 45min) to bachelor's students on UAVs (Drones). It was a hybrid session with 10-15 students in the classroom and 20-25 online participants - making it both an exciting and a unique learning experience for me.

While preparing for this lecture, I made it a priority to draw from my past project experiences to give students real-world examples of UAV applications which is reflected in my lecture title, 'From Coastlines to Railways: Practical Insights into UAV applications'. I shared insights from projects like sand dune mapping, railway line surveys for wildlife safety, object detection on UAV imagery, and environmental monitoring. This approach helped connect the theoretical aspects of UAVs to tangible problems and practical solutions.

Grateful for this opportunity and the positive interaction with such an enthusiastic group of students! Looking forward to many more teaching experiences where I can share knowledge, inspire learning, and grow alongside.

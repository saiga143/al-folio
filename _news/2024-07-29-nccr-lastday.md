🌅 29 July 2024 marked my last working day at the National Centre for Coastal Research (NCCR), Ministry of Earth Sciences, Government of India.

As I reflect on my journey at NCCR, I am filled with gratitude for the experience and opportunities that shaped my professional growth. I was fortunate to visit numerous coastal states and union territories (UTs) and cover hundreds of kilometers of coastal stretches, from coasts to mangroves, sand dunes, islands, ports and harbors. 🌊🏝️These varied landscapes provided invaluable real-world perspectives that deeply informed my work.

During my tenure, I had the privilege of being involved in developing shoreline management plans for the states and UTs I covered (Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Kerala), a process that required close collaboration with various stakeholders from government departments and local inhabitants. The work was challenging but immensely rewarding, knowing that our efforts were contributing to sustainable coastal management 🛠️📜.

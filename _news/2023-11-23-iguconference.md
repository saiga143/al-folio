Excited to share that our research "An Integrated Methodology for Effective Management of a Multifarious Coast" was recently presented (23-11-2023) at the Indian Geophysical Union's (IGU) Diamond Jubilee Convention focused on Coastal Hazards. 🗻 🌋 🌊 ⛵ 

Ms. Mridula GM, representing our research team, delivered an engaging poster presentation that sparked valuable dialogue on bridging the gap between geoscientific research and societal applications. 👩‍💼 🌏 🚀 

The feedback from session chairs and delegates, including prominent scientists and academicians, has been very positive 🤝 . Our approach to enhancing policy formulation 📜 and coastal management practices was particularly well-received, being noted for its practical application in aiding decision-making processes. 🌟 🤗

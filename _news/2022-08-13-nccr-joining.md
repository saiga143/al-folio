New beginnings!

I am happy to share that I have started a new position today as Project Scientist I (Remote sensing) at National Center for Coastal Research(NCCR), Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES), Government of India (GoI). 

I will be working in the ‘Coastal Processes and Shoreline Management (CPSM)’ group cluster headed by Mr. V Ramanathan (Scientist E). 

I am excited to explore the applications of GIS and Remote Sensing in the field of coastal studies. And also to interact and learn from the vast diverse and experienced team of NCCR.

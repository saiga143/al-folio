🎉 Exciting News! 🎉

📘Today marks the beginning of a new chapter in my professional life as I start my full-time role as a Doktorand (Doctoral Student) in Risk and Environmental Studies at Karlstad University, Sweden. I am eager to dive into my research📚 and contribute to the vibrant academic community here.

🙏A heartfelt thank you to the interview panel for an engaging conversation and the decision committee for entrusting me with this role. Your confidence in my abilities is greatly appreciated!🌟

🤗I am extremely grateful to my supervisors, Stefanos Georganos, Jan Haas, Konstantinos Karagiorgos and Lars Nyberg, who have been exceptionally welcoming. Thank you, Stef and Jan, for showing me around and helping me settle in at the university and in Karlstad.

👏Special thanks to Sofia Hallerbäck for her constant support and assistance with everything I needed to get started. You have been a tremendous help!

📋I also want to acknowledge Anette and Erika from the administrative and HR teams for their meticulous preparation and support, ensuring a smooth transition into my new role.

🌍Finally, working alongside Stef, Jan, Konstantinos, and Lars promises to be an inspiring journey. I am looking forward to the amazing experiences and learning opportunities that lie ahead in the coming years.

🥂Here's to new beginnings and the exciting road ahead! 🚀
